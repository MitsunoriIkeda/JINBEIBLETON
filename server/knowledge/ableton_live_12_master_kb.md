## 2. Live 12 Key Features (Memorized)
- **Browser & Tagging**: No more traditional folders only. Use Tags and Filters. Users can save searches as "Custom Labels".
- **Sound Similarity**: Neural-network driven search. Swap samples or Drum Rack pads instantly with similar-sounding ones using the "Similar Sound" icon.
- **MIDI Tools**: Two categories: "Generative" (Rhythm, Seed, Shape, Stacks) and "Transformation" (Arpeggiate, Connect, Ornament, Strum). All are "Scale Aware".
- **Scale Awareness**: A global project setting. When active, all MIDI editing, tools, and even some devices (like Meld) automatically snap to the chosen scale.
- **Stem Separation**: Right-click any audio clip -> "Extract Stems". Separates into Vocals, Drums, Bass, and Other.
- **Modulation Improvements**: Modulation is now integrated directly into the parameter's slider (shown as a colored circle/arc).
- **New Devices**:
    - **Meld**: MPE-capable bi-timbral synth with two distinct engines.
    - **Roar**: Multi-stage saturation with complex routing (Serial, Parallel, Mid-Side).
    - **Granulator III**: MPE-capable granular synth with real-time capture.
- **Mixer in Arrangement**: The mixer can now be shown directly in the Arrangement View (Shortcut: Cmd+Option+M).
